Useful to know

Will a project reference become a confirmed price?

No. Published project prices are labelled as references. Enter the price of the specific unit and identify it before marking it as a unit price. This remains your input, not independent verification.

Can I compare offers with missing data?

Yes. Missing area hides only the price-per-m² calculation. Missing costs prevent a full total; known amounts remain visible. Nothing missing becomes zero.

Can I compare USD, THB and IDR?

Yes, each offer retains its original currency. Different currencies are not converted or ranked.

Where are my notes stored?

Your draft stays in this browser. Board files include your inputs and notes. You decide whether to share them. Switching language preserves your board.
