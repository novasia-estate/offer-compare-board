A short field guide

How to read this board

A blank cost is unknown, not zero. Enter 0 only when you have established that no amount applies. “Known subtotal” is incomplete until all upfront costs are supplied. Offers in different currencies stay separate. Unit prices require comparable area definitions; note whether an area is net, gross or land.

Milestones cover the purchase price only. They must reconcile exactly, and exclude extra fees. The date view is a payment deadline summary, not a statement of payments already made. Arithmetic uses two decimal places; use the precision and settlement rules required by your actual contract.

A comparison you can verify

Our synthetic Thailand example combines a THB 3,500,000 price, THB 40,000 closing costs and THB 180,000 furnishing: THB 3,720,000 upfront. Its 80 m² purchase-price unit cost is THB 43,750/m². The second offer deliberately has an unknown closing fee, so a complete total is not shown.

These figures demonstrate the software. They are not live offers or market estimates.

Who is it for?

Property buyers comparing written offers; agents preparing a shortlist with a client; and analysts who need to keep assumptions beside their figures. NovAsia built the board around purchase decisions in Cambodia, Thailand and Bali. The calculations can also be used with offers elsewhere when the stated currency and area conventions are suitable.

Before you use the board

Does the board upload my property offers?

The tool processes inputs in the browser. Optional browser saving uses local storage. Exported JSON files contain your notes and offer details, so choose carefully before sharing them with anyone.

Can I compare USD, THB and IDR?

Yes, the original currency label stays with each offer. The board does not fetch exchange rates or produce a cross-currency ranking. Convert separately with a dated rate if your decision requires a common currency.

Does it calculate tax or rental returns?

No automatic tax rates, yields or availability are supplied. Enter costs you have obtained for the exact offer. The board organises the purchase comparison; it does not replace the contract or professional advice.

Can I inspect or run the code myself?

Yes. The GitHub repository contains the source, tests and MIT licence. Open index.html locally, or use the self-contained standalone.html edition. Node.js is needed only to run the automated tests or rebuild that edition.
